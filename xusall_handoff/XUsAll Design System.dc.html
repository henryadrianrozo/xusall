Design system, v1

XUsAll

The rules every XUsAll property is built from. One canvas, one typeface, one spectrum. Everything here exists because it earns its place. If a decision isn't in this document, default to doing less.

Brand
Wordmark XUsAll

Set in the system typeface at 500, tracking -0.04em. There is no logomark and none should be drawn. Child products extend the same construction: XUsDemocracy, XUsNotes, XUsCurrency, XUsContacts.

X is a variable Connect. Empower. Support. Teach. Protect.

The X is the open slot. Some verb, applied to us, applied to all. The spectrum is the visual form of that variable: the full range of what X can be, and of the people it is for.

Product marks
XUsDemocracy XUsNotes XUsCurrency XUsContacts

Each product takes one or two colors from the spectrum. Us always stays white. Colors are drawn from the palette below, never invented.

Color

Dark only. There is no light theme and no theme toggle. True black is the canvas; near-black is the only elevation. There is no single brand accent. The accent is the spectrum, and it appears at most three times on any page.

The spectrum
● #FF4D6D ● #FF9F43 ● #FFD93D ● #4ADE80 ● #38BDF8 ● #A78BFA

Slightly desaturated, never toy-like. Always ordered as listed, always looping back to the first color so a gradient has no seam. Permitted uses: the 1px page bar, the X in the wordmark, and one hairline rule per page. Never as a fill behind text, never on a headline, never as a background wash.

Type

One family: Helvetica Neue, falling back to Arial. Weights 400 and 500 only. Tight tracking at display sizes does most of the work. Sentence case everywhere except eyebrows.

Space, layout, shape
8px scale

8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96 / 120 / 160. Nothing off the scale.

Layout

Content column max 1120px. Gutters 24px mobile, 48px desktop. Section padding 120 to 160px desktop, 80 to 96px mobile. Sections separated by a 1px white 8% rule, never by a heavier divider or a background change.

Shape and elevation

6px small, 12px cards, pill for status. Borders do the separation work. No shadows, no glows, no glass, no gradients on surfaces.

Components

A small set, used everywhere. No icons anywhere in the system.

Motion and accessibility

The spectrum bar

1px tall, fixed to the top of the viewport, above everything. Translated on a 60 second linear loop, seamless, transform only. It should be barely perceptible. Frozen, not removed, under reduced motion.

Everything else

150 to 250ms on cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1). One fade and rise of 14px for section entrances, once, no re-trigger. Anchor navigation scrolls smoothly. Nothing bounces.

Accessibility floor

WCAG AA on all text. Tertiary is #7C8492, which clears 4.5:1 on black at any size. Focus is a 1px primary-text outline offset 4px, never a spectrum ring. Semantic landmarks, real headings, no layout shift from the bar.

Voice

Quiet confidence. Plainspoken, unsentimental, a little idealistic, never corporate and never preachy. Short sentences. Second person for product copy, first person plural only for brand moments. No exclamation marks, no em dashes, no emoji.
